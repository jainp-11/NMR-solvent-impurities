Impurity

Shifts of one impurity in every solvent — useful for choosing a solvent that moves it off your signals. The current solvent is highlighted; click a column heading to switch to it.

Data: Fulmer, G. R. et al. Organometallics 2010, 29, 2176–2179 (Tables 1 and 2), δ in ppm at 298 K. “—” = not reported / not observed. Multiplicities: s singlet, d doublet, t triplet, q quartet, sept septet, m multiplet, br broad; J in Hz. Shifts of OH/NH and water vary with concentration, temperature and other solutes. The plots are simulated: first-order splitting from the listed J values, 1H peak areas proportional to the number of protons with every impurity at the same concentration, signals reported as “m” drawn with a generic shape, and 13C lines all given equal height.
